# MLWA submission reproducibility release v1.0.2

This release corresponds to the final submission candidate of:

**Cost-Aware Reliability Signal Selection for Finite-Choice Small Language Models: A Locked Validation Study**

## Included

- Final manuscript and supplementary LaTeX sources and PDFs
- Institutional email addresses for all eight authors on the title pages
- Final locked-validation outputs
- 50,000-replicate crossed model/dataset bootstrap analysis
- Leave-one-model-out and leave-one-dataset-out sensitivity
- Alternative validation-objective and equal-search-budget analyses
- Feature-schema audit and strict output-only Gemma/Llama sensitivity
- Cost-utility and failure-prevalence summaries
- Reproducibility manifest, environment files, and citation metadata
